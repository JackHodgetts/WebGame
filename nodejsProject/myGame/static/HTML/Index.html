This is the home page for the web puzzle game Maze Ascendancy!!!

You have been trapped in a magical tower filled with mazes on each floor. Can you ascend the tower and solve the puzzles within the mazes to escape??!!

You play as an adventurer who got trapped in a tower fill of mazes. Each floor has a more complex maze with some puzzles. You will have to find the buttons that are around the maze to open the exit to maze to the next floor of this magical tower. If you are unable to solve the maze and the puzzle in the timer limit then you will be trapped forever in this tower.
